Our story

Small beginnings.
A bigger idea of care.

AM SRI began in Virudhunagar in 2012 with a love for aquarium life and a belief that pet shopping should feel personal. Since then, our shelves have grown to welcome dogs, cats, birds, fish, and the people who love them.

We choose the things we would want for our own pets: reliable food, useful accessories, and everyday essentials that make life together a little easier.

"We are here for the everyday moments that make a pet feel at home."

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers — if you need more, our team is always happy to help in store or over the phone.

Do you stock food and supplies for all kinds of pets?
Yes. We carry products for dogs, cats, fish, birds and small pets like rabbits and guinea pigs. If we don't have something in stock, we'll do our best to source it for you.
Can you help me set up a new aquarium?
Absolutely — aquariums are where AM SRI began. We'll guide you on tank size, filtration, lighting, water conditioning and the right fish to start with, so your setup thrives from day one.
Are all your products genuine and fresh?
Every product on our shelves is sourced from authorised brands and trusted suppliers. We rotate stock regularly so food and supplements are always within their best-before dates.
Do you offer advice if my pet has a specific issue?
We're pet people first. Share what's going on and we'll suggest practical, everyday solutions — though for medical concerns we always recommend consulting a qualified veterinarian.
Can I order online and pick up in store?
Yes. Browse and order through our online shop, then collect your essentials from our Virudhunagar store at a time that suits you. Call ahead and we'll have it ready.
