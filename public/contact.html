Good to know

Contact & visit FAQs.

Quick answers before you reach out — and if we've missed something, just send us a message above.

What are your store hours?
We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and Sundays from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The live "Open now" badge on this page shows our current status.
How quickly will I get a reply?
We aim to respond to every message within 24 hours. For anything urgent, calling the store directly is the fastest way to reach us.
Where are you located?
You'll find us at Professors Colony, Anna Nagar, Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu 626001. Use the map below for directions, or call us if you need help finding the shop.
Do you take orders over the phone?
Yes. Call us with your list and we'll prepare your order for pickup or arrange delivery where possible. Payment can be made in store or online as arranged.
Can I get help choosing the right product?
Always. Tell us about your pet — age, breed, size and any preferences — and we'll recommend food, accessories and care products that genuinely suit them.
